Pancakes

Description

Pancakes are a popular breakfast food made from a smooth, pourable starch-based batter cooked on a hot, flat surface like a griddle or frying pan. They are universally loved for their comforting taste, simple ingredients, and versatile nature.

Ingredients

  • Dry Ingredients:
    • All-Purpose Flour (Maida): 1 cup
    • Baking Powder: 2 tsp
    • Sugar: 2 tbsp
    • Salt: ¼ tsp

  • Wet Ingredients
    • Milk: ¾ cup to 1 cup (room temperature)
    • Melted Butter or Oil:2 tbsp (plus extra for greasing the pan)
    • Egg: 1 large (or substitute with ¼ cup yogurt/curd for an eggless version)
    • Vanilla Extract: 1 tsp

Steps

  • Mix Dry and Wet Ingredients Separately:
    1. In a large mixing bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, sugar, and salt.
    2. In a separate bowl, whisk together the milk, egg (or yogurt), melted butter, and vanilla extract until well combined.

  • Combine to Form Batter:
    1. Pour the wet mixture into the dry ingredients.
    2. Gently fold together with a spatula or whisk just until combined. Do not overmix—a few small lumps are completely fine and keep the pancakes fluffy.
    3. Let the batter rest for 5-10 minutes so the baking powder activates.

  • Cook the Pancakes:
    1. Heat a non-stick pan or skillet over medium-low heat and grease lightly with a little butter.
    2. Pour about ¼ cup of batter onto the hot pan for each pancake.
    3. Cook for 2-3 minutes until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look set.
    4. Flip gently with a spatula and cook the other side for another 1-2 minutes until golden brown.
