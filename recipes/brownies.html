Brownies

Description

Brownies are a rich, baked chocolate dessert named for their deep brown color. They are a beloved cross between a dense cake and a soft fudge, traditionally baked in a square pan and cut into bars.

Ingredients

  • Chocolate Base:
    • Unsalted Butter: ½ cup (115g)
    • Dark Chocolate Chunks or Chips: ½ cup (approx. 100g)
    • Cocoa Powder: ⅓ cup (unsweetened)

  • Dry & Wet Ingredients:
    • All-Purpose Flour (Maida): ½ cup
    • Granulated Sugar: 1 cup
    • Eggs: 2 large (or substitute with ½ cup condensed milk / ¼ cup yogurt for eggless)
    • Vanilla Extract: 1 tsp
    • Salt: ¼ tsp
    • Chocolate Chips (Optional): ½ cup for extra texture

Steps

  • Melt Chocolate & Butter:
    1. Pre-heat your oven to 180°C (350°F) and line an 8x8 inch square baking pan with parchment paper.
    2. Combine the butter and dark chocolate chunks in a heatproof bowl.
    3. Melt over a double boiler or microwave in 30-second bursts, stirring until smooth and glossy.

  • Whip Eggs and Sugar for Crackly Top:
    1. In a separate bowl, vigorously whisk the eggs (or egg substitute) and sugar together for 2–3 minutes until slightly pale and foamy. This step creates the shiny, crackly crust.
    2. Whisk in the vanilla extract, then slowly pour in the warm melted chocolate-butter mixture while whisking continuously.

  • Fold Dry Ingredients:
    1. Sift the flour, cocoa powder, and salt into the wet batter.
    2. Gently fold with a spatula just until the flour disappears. Do not overmix.
    3. Stir in extra chocolate chips if using, then pour the batter into the lined baking pan and smooth the top.
  • Bake and Cool
    1. Bake at 180°C (350°F) for 25-28 minutes.
    2. Check with a toothpick inserted into the center: it should come out with a few moist crumbs, not wet batter.
    3. Cool completely in the pan for at least 30-45 minutes before slicing into squares.
